About

My name is Gwenhwyfar. I'm an artist working across painting, drawing, quiltmaking, weaving, net art, singing, electronic music, and the bağlama, in a lifelong quest to touch the divine—to find my way home.

I was taught very young that being an artist was categorically not possible, like being a wizard or a fairy, so I spent decades flirting with art indirectly. Always drawn to beauty and truth, to the grotesque and the sublime, I followed spiritual ecstasy, designed dance exorcisms, facilitated artist cohorts, and experimented across many forms—but have only recently dared to call myself an artist.

My aesthetic sensibilities have long been shaped by Central Asia, outsider art, and folk art—worlds that exist outside and between established categories, and that feel, strangely, like the home I've been yearning for. I am interested in the places where categories dissolve: between sacred and mundane, ancient and contemporary, highbrow and low. For similar reasons, I adore the Internet, and have been quite online since I was a child. I have found my people on the Internet. Welcome to my corner of it!
